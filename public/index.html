Good To Know

Questions We Hear Daily

Do you sell wholesale and retail?

Yes. We sell to individuals, installers and resellers. Message us the quantity you need for a wholesale price.

How do I place an order?

Add items to your cart, tap “Send Order on WhatsApp”, and we will reply to confirm stock, price and delivery.

Can I see a video of the product first?

Yes. Many products have a video on their page. For any other item, tap “Ask video” and we will send you one on WhatsApp.

Do you deliver outside Lagos?

Yes. We deliver within Lagos and send to other states. Contact us to arrange delivery to your location.

How do I know what size to buy?

Tell us what you want to power, such as lights, fridge, TV or pump. We will recommend the right panels, battery and inverter for your budget.

How do I pay?

You can pay by bank transfer or at the shop. We confirm the payment details with you on WhatsApp before delivery.
